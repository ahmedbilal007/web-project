Club Road, Islamabad Indoor Royal Ballroom

About The Venue

The Royal Palm Grand Ballroom represents the pinnacle of luxury event architecture in the twin cities. Featuring imported Swarovski crystal chandeliers suspended from towering 26-foot ceilings, bespoke hand-tufted Persian carpeting, and professional acoustic dampening, it is designed for grand society weddings, diplomatic galas, and premier banquets. The hall features an integrated 40ft LED backdrop stage, private bridal prep sanctuary, and private VIP foyer for dignitary arrivals.
