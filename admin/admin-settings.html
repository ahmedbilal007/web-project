Permission guide for the future backend. This public preview does not authorize or restrict any user.

The backend must check permissions on every protected page and action.
Role Permitted Functions Restrictions
Staff View bookings, booking details and permitted staff information. Update status and internal notes when supported. No account creation, account status changes, password changes or protected settings.
Manager Staff functions, quotations and permitted catalogue or booking operations. No Super Admin account management or Super Admin-only staff management.
Super Admin All administrative sections, staff creation, password changes, account status changes and restricted settings. The predefined Super Admin account must remain protected.
